About

52 years in business and still going strong

The Quest Team is an organization of highly experienced professionals offering high-impact, semi-custom training processes and sales and marketing consulting services to high technology industries.

National award: Certificate of Achievement in Education from the American Association of Association Executives, for “Best United States program delivered through a large industry association.”National award: Certificate of Achievement in Education (American Association of Association Executives)

The Quest Team is an organization of highly experienced professionals offering

  • High impact, semi-custom training processes for the development of sales, marketing and customer teams
  • Sales and marketing consulting services to high technology industries

Quest Team members are highly qualified. Each has had a successful career as a senior executive in a sales and marketing career path. All have in-depth experience in the areas in which they are consulting and/or training. This experience, combined with Quest's knowledge of learning and behavior change, provides you with quality solutions and defined payoffs at a highly favorable cost/performance ratio.

Quest Team knows you. We have worked extensively with management, sales, marketing, engineering and manufacturing teams in some 23 industries. Our broad experience means innovative, timely solutions that are "real world" to your people.

Quest Team approaches are uniquely tuned to your organization. We listen to you and then design a semi-custom solution for the challenges you face. When seeking sales or marketing consultation we can rapidly bring to bear more than 200 person years of in-depth experience. When seeking training and development, we have more than 50 modules and hundreds of exercises at our disposal to design a process that is a precise fit for your organization.

  • Teams in some 23 industries; the highest concentration in semiconductors and semiconductor equipment & materials
  • Courses presented as a joint venture with SEMI
  • More than 50 training modules and hundreds of exercises
